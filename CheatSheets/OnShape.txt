-- Keyboard shortcuts
	# Top level	  
	* N: Make view normal to plane selected or sketch being worked
	  on.
	* Shift-S: Start a sketch; you'll be prompted for a plane or
	  face.
	* P: Hide or show all planes at once
	
	# In sketch
	* L: Create a line.
	* Q: Define the current behavior as "construction".
	* <shift>: Turns off automatic inferencing while held.
	* <shift>: While constraints are shown, holding shift keeps
	  them visible as you move the mouse.
	* i: Coincident constraint
	* Shift-o: Concentric constraint
	* b: Parallel constraint
	* t: Tangent constraint
	* h/v: Horizontal/vertical constraint.
	* Shift-l: Perpendicular constraint
	* e: Equal constraint
	* Shift-m: Midpoint constraint
	* Shift-k: Normal constraint
	* Shift-g: Pierce (attach point/curve with an edge
	  intersecting sketch plane)
	* Shift-q: Symmetric constraint
	* Shift-j: Fix (make immovable) a particular entity.
	* Shift-u: Same curvature
	* O: Offset
